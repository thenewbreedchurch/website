Our Story

From a simple idea to a vibrant youth movement impacting lives across Lagos and beyond.

How New Breed Began

In 2020, a void emerged for young adults transitioning out of the Teens church. Too old for one, and not quite at home in the main sanctuary, they needed a place to grow spiritually and serve purposefully.

With the support of Pastor Idowu Iluyomade and Pastor Gbenga Olaniyan, New Breed was born — a bridge for young believers discovering who they are in Christ and what they're called to do.

Worship is Our Lifestyle

We don't just worship on Sundays — worship is how we live. Every service is a moment to encounter God, build community, and be equipped for the week ahead.

Our worship is youthful, spirit-led, and transformative.

Our Mission & Vision

Mission: To raise a generation of young believers who are passionate about Christ, equipped for service, and committed to transforming their communities through the Gospel.

Vision: A thriving community where young adults discover their identity in Christ, develop their gifts, and deploy into their God-given purpose with boldness and faith.
